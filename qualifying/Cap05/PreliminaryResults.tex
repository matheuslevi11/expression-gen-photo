\section{Threats to Validity}\label{sec:res-threats}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Label--evaluation circularity.} py-feat provides both the training labels and the primary metric. Mitigated by the independent MediaPipe measurement (Section~\ref{subsec:eval-circularity}), which agreed, but not eliminated.
    \item \textbf{Demographic bias.} Ramp $r$ is $0.83$ for female prompts against $0.71$ for male prompts. The prior art shows the opposite skew, which localises the cause to the \gls{mead} training distribution rather than to AU-conditioned generation in general. \todo[inline]{Confirm the FineFace per-gender figures against lab note 2026-07-21.}
    \item \textbf{Lab-domain training data.} A single dataset, a single emotion category, frontal views, controlled lighting. Generalisation to in-the-wild portraits is untested.
    \item \textbf{Single control axis.} Only AU12 is conditioned; the prior art controls twelve action units and their combinations.
    \item \textbf{Single training run.} One seed, one checkpoint; no variance estimate over training runs.
    \item \textbf{No evidence yet that training drives the capability.} The training-step dose--response experiment is pending (Cap06). \todo[inline]{Intermediate checkpoints are the raw material and must not be deleted before this runs.}
\end{itemize}
